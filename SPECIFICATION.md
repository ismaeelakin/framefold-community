# How a feature becomes buildable

The active gate is specification review. The application has not acquired new tools merely because the plans are more detailed.

## What is recorded

The private engineering set contains **48 proposed capability packets**, including the exceptional workflows and six separate 3D-modelling areas. It also contains shared engine contracts, a decision/experiment register, 149 acceptance fixture recipes and 12 mathematical vectors. Public scope and dependencies are summarized in [FEATURES.md](FEATURES.md), [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) and [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md). The complete implementation design and source are maintained privately.

Each capability packet records:

1. The artist's task, the audience output and the release boundary.
2. Document fields, identities, references, validation and migration requirements.
3. Interactions, keyboard access, empty states, cancellation, offline behaviour and recovery.
4. Algorithms, numerical conventions and module interfaces.
5. Resource limits, import/privacy boundaries and the zero-spend condition.
6. Fixture inputs and required results, including failures and data preservation.
7. Dependencies, unresolved choices and entry/exit evidence.

## What remains open

Archive/container libraries, codecs and muxers, geometry kernels, actual device baselines and hosted providers are not selected or validated yet. Exact production validators and fixture assets must be completed for the relevant release. A design that names a research algorithm is not evidence that it works for the intended artist task.

## Review and admission

First reconcile schemas, coordinate/time conventions, dependencies and failure behaviour. Materialize fixtures with safe, licensed assets and recorded versions. Resolve the selected capability's technical choices. Then record whether it is ready for implementation, ready only for an experiment, or deferred.

Before release, record the source version, browser/OS/device, fixture inputs, measured results, exported artifacts and known deviations. No data-loss or authorization defect is accepted as a documentation footnote.

The first current-alpha creative probes follow the specification review and may change the proposed designs. They do not justify skipping specifications or assuming artist demand.

## Research packets

Research has a hypothesis, alternatives, bounded inputs, measurements and a stop condition. Outcomes can be a limited implementation, revised design, deferral or no-go. In particular, selective topology review must remain conservative when identity or dependencies are ambiguous. It must not silently merge invalid geometry to appear advanced.

## Contributions at this stage

You can challenge an assumption, suggest an edge case, describe a real production workflow, improve a guide or propose an experiment. Reference a capability ID where possible and distinguish observed behaviour from a proposed feature. Private-source access is not required for this work and is not granted automatically.

Use [a research proposal](https://github.com/ismaeelakin/framefold-community/issues/new?template=research_proposal.yml) for a focused experiment. No participation counts, benchmark results or completion claims should be invented.
